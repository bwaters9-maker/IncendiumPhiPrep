# Design assets

Source images and reference art for the project. Nothing here is referenced by
`index.html` yet — these are inputs for design work, not shipped assets.

## assets/waterstock-ticket.jpg

Waterstock event ticket mockup. 2576x924 JPEG.

Layout, left to right:

- **Stub** — cream panel, "ADMIT ONE", QR code with a longhorn/cow glyph
  centered in it, serial `# 1969-0818-JW`, terms line `GA · LAWN · $18.00 ADV`.
  Perforated edge separating it from the main body.
- **Center panel** — Woodstock-style "PEACE LOVE MUSIC" roundel in orange /
  yellow / magenta / green psychedelic lettering over a blue-violet tie-dye
  field.
- **Right panel** — "Waterstock" wordmark in a beveled serif over a mandolin /
  guitar body, tagline "Peace & Music at the Click of a Button".
- **Border** — red-orange textured frame with repeating micro-text
  ("WATERSTOCK MUSIC & ARTS FAIR", "ADMIT ONE") along all four edges.
